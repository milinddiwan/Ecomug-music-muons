# Ecomug-music-muons
Combines the calculation of ecomug surface muons and music deep underground or under water transport. 

Currently it is setup for 200 m of water.  

The program muon_prop_200m produced a file of 10000 surviving muons at 200 meters and reports their statistics.  

muon_range produces the ranges of muon  in rock.
